OFFLINE DAoC REMASTERED - CREDITS AND SPECIAL THANKS
===================================================

Dark Age of Camelot
  Mythic Entertainment - the original developers who made Dark Age of Camelot in 2001,

  Midgard and Hibernia.
  Broadsword Online Games - for keeping Camelot alive all these years, and for keeping

Offline DAoC
  u/zomboden and everyone on Reddit who made and shared Offline DAoC, the single-player

OpenDAoC and Dawn of Light
  The OpenDAoC team and contributors, and the Dawn of Light (DOL) project before them -

Claude, by Anthropic

And thank you
  To everyone who still remembers training mobs to the zone line, holding the Emain
